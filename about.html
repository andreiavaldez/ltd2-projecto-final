About

I’m a Service and UX/UI Designer based in Lisbon. I’m currently studying at the Faculty of Fine Arts (University of Lisbon) and previously specialised in UX/UI at EDIT.

Today at ARTE — the agency redesigning Portugal's public digital services — I work on platforms that scale to millions. Before that: film industry, media monitoring, nonprofits. Different contexts, same question: how can we design solutions that are useful, clear, and authentic?
